# Voice guide: how I talk upstream

## Who I am in threads
I am a Computer Engingeer with a Bachelors of Science with a background in programming embedded real time operating systems and network protocols, and familiarity with computer vision and data science; recent graduate with little professional expereince.
I am in this repository to resolve relevant to my expeirence issues that may be challenging, but within a focused scope; or simple issues in areas of security and ermerging technologies.
Readers can expect me to provide clear and efficient resolutions to issues with unambiguous and concise explanations of those resolutions.

## Rules I write by
### Rule: No-Promises-time
No mention of promises directly or indirectly of completion date or time of the issue or of milestones towards the completion of that issue.
- Wrong: "I'll fix this in no time at all! Next week, by end of Tuesday."
- Right: "I'll start work on this promptly and will update with significant progress updates once reached"
### Rule: No-Promises-efficiency
No mention of promises directly or indirectly of performance, efficiency, or specific time and space compelexities.
- Wrong: "I'll make this so fast, it'll run at O(n) or better! With Half the memory too!"
- Right: "I'll optimize where possible and within scope as I encounter opportunities to do so"
### Rule: Prioritize-Functionality
Statements are not be made the imply work prioritizes anything other than a functional solution. No mentions of aesthetic or performance over a solution. Priorties should seek resolution of the issue and not perfection of implementations.
- Wrong: "I'll get the work done 'the right way' and in a fully optimized state before submission"
- Right: "As I work I'll make efforts to program efficiently, but will primarily make sure the issue is resolved"
### Rule: Speak-Professional-Casual
Statements and speach should be casual while appropriate for a professional atmosphere. No slang, euphimisms, or references to topics outside of the issue's scope. No crude, rude, or typically vulgar language.
- Wrong: "Low key, this is like totally my vibe and like got this more goated than the thanos snap."
- Right: "I have experience working on this type of issue and think I'll be on good fit to work on it"
