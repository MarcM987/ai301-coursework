# Evidence guide: where evidence lives in a plan package

## Diagnosis and grounding
Where it lives:
- Eval: the repro evidence, including the steps, expected, and actual, along with the plan's diagnosis
- Live: the plan's diagnosis section and the repro evidence on the issue

What good looks like:
The plan's stated cause of diagnosis cites and does not contradict the repro evidence

## Scope
Where it lives:
- Eval: the repro evidence and the plan's scope and changes sections
- Live: the issue's description, the repro evidence, and the plan's scope and changes sections

What good looks like:
The plan is relevent only to the issue and repro evidence, with no non-relevant functions adjusted without explanation as to why it is relevant to the issue.

## Executability
Where it lives:
- Eval: the plan and its mentioned files or changes
- Live: the plan and its mentioned files or changes

What good looks like:
The plan can be directly followed by someone other than the author with clear and directly intentioned goals.

## Test plan
Where it lives:
- Eval: the plan's test plan section compared with the repro evidence
- Live: the plan's test plan section compared with the repro evidence

What good looks like:
Effectively or similarly re-implements the same reproduction steps in the repro evidence.

## Honesty
Where it lives:
- Eval: the plan's diagnosis, changes, and plan comment
- Live: the plan's diagnosis, changes, and plan comment

What good looks like:
Any uncertainties in the plan are clearly addressed and deviation from the repro are clearly explained.

## Comms
Where it lives:
- Eval: the plan and plan comment along with the thread highlights, repo facts, contribution policies, and ai policies
- Live: the plan and plan comment along with the issue thread, the repo contribution policies, and ai policies

What good looks like:
The comment and plan adhere to the contribution and ai policies and disclosures as well as any additional direction provided by the maintainer
